Before you refer an applicant

Most funders ask for the same core documents. Get these from the applicant first — it saves a wasted application.

  • Valid SA ID / passport (all owners & directors)
  • CIPC company registration certificate (or proof of informal business)
  • Business plan / funding proposal
  • Latest financial statements or management accounts
  • 6 months bank statements
  • Tax clearance / SARS compliance status
  • B-BBEE certificate or affidavit (where applicable)
  • Quotes for the assets / project being funded
  • Proof of premises / lease agreement
  • ID copies of all shareholders (for empowerment funds)
Compiled for internal staff use. All details verified against official sources, Aug 2026. Funding programmes change — always confirm current criteria on the institution's website before referring an applicant.
